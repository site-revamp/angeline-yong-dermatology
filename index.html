Before your visit

Which unit should I contact?

Call 6252 0026 for cosmetic and medical enquiries or 6592 1311 for surgical enquiries. If you’re unsure, ask the team on WhatsApp.

Can I request an appointment directly?

Yes. You can contact the clinic directly. If you plan to use insurance, ask your insurer about any referral requirements.

What are the Saturday hours?

Consultations run from 8am to 12.30pm. Therapist treatments run from 8am to 5pm. The clinic will confirm availability for your visit.
