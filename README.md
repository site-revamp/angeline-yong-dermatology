# Angeline Yong Dermatology — Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

Angeline Yong Dermatology Pte. Ltd. is a private specialist dermatology clinic at Gleneagles Medical Centre in Singapore. Founded by Dr Angeline Yong, the practice provides medical, cosmetic and surgical care for skin, hair and nail concerns. The homepage is intended to help prospective and existing patients understand the clinic’s services and find the appropriate route to request a consultation or contact the relevant unit.

The redesign makes consultation access more prominent, clarifies the available treatment pathways and presents contact details in a more useful, unit-specific way.

## Main Improvements

1. **A clear consultation action in the opening section**
   - **Changed:** Added **Request a consultation** to the hero section and navigation.
   - **Addresses:** The previous homepage opened with an image carousel, while the appointment section appeared farther down the page. The new placement makes the consultation action visible earlier.

2. **Distinct, clearly labelled treatment pathways**
   - **Changed:** Replaced repeated **Learn More** prompts with six clearly labelled paths, including direct links to nail biopsy information, Mohs surgery and benign lesion surgery.
   - **Addresses:** Repeated generic links made it harder for visitors to distinguish treatment options and identify relevant information.

3. **Contact details identified by clinic unit**
   - **Changed:** Paired **#02-04/05** with **6252 0026** for cosmetic and medical enquiries, and **#03-02** with **6592 1311** for surgical enquiries. A mobile bottom bar provides access to both phone numbers and WhatsApp.
   - **Addresses:** The previous appointment section listed both phone numbers without identifying which unit each served, making it less clear which number patients should use.

## Design Improvements

- **Clearer visual hierarchy:** The consultation action is brought into the hero and navigation rather than being left farther down the page.
- **More scannable service navigation:** Six labelled treatment paths make the available routes easier to distinguish than repeated generic links.
- **More useful contact presentation:** Phone numbers are grouped with their corresponding unit and address details.
- **Improved mobile access:** A persistent bottom bar keeps phone and WhatsApp contact options accessible to mobile visitors.
